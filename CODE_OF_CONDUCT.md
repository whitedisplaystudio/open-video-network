# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the work,
not about the person doing it.

## Expected

* Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
* Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully. Review comments are about
  code.
* Say what you mean plainly, and prefer clarity over cleverness — many people
  here are not writing in their first language.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment of any kind, public or private.
* Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
* Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good faith
may be removed from the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not meet this standard,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
